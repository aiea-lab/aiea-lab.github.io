Miriam Tekle is a third-year undergraduate double majoring in Computer Science and Cognitive Science at UC Santa Cruz, and an auditor in the AIEA Lab under Prof. Leilani H. Gilpin. Her research interests include explainable AI and AI security, particularly understanding why AI systems make the decisions they do and how they can fail. Through her cognitive science coursework, she worked on a survey study of academic pressure and AI reliance among college students. She is also interested in cybersecurity and is currently competing in the 2026 NSA Codebreaker Challenge. Outside of research, she plays several instruments and rides motorcycles.
