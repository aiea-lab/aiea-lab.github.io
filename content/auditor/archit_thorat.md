Archit Thorat is a third-year B.E. student in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at PES Modern College of Engineering in Pune, India.

He is interested in neurosymbolic AI and interpretability, especially breaking AI-written text into individual claims and checking each one against its sources. He started Daily City News, a local news brand in Pune, and built the news engine his team uses every day to find, write and publish stories.

He has also written a solo paper on making language models smaller. At the AIEA Lab he is starting on the LLM + Logic track.
