Tactile Map Annotation: On a tactile map, a building under the finger is an unnamed shape. Our tool gives it a name in Braille. Ten blind and low-vision readers compared labeled and unlabeled prints. By linking the 3D model with a real-world map, annotation becomes easy and sharable across map modalities and places.

**Dates:** March 2026 – Present
