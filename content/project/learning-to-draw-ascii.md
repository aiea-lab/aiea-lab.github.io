People sketch to reason about space. We ask whether language models can do the same, and find that LLMs read ASCII layouts better than they draw them, and that training a model only to draw these layouts improves its spatial reasoning from text alone.

**Dates:** September 2025 – September 2026

**Venue:** COLM 2026
