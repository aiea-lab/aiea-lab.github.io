Creighton is a third-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science advised by [Prof. Leilani Gilpin](../leilani/) at UC Santa Cruz. His research explores the development of more efficient and sustainable AI agents. With work at the intersection of reinforcement learning and large language models, he employs novel compression techniques to extend AI efficiency gains into the agentic domain for solving sequential decision-making problems, namely Markov decision processes (MDPs).

Outside of research, Creighton loves exploring the outdoors, primarily through cycling and rock climbing.
